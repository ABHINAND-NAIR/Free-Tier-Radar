How we judge

  • Free forever — usable indefinitely on the free plan without paying. Limits exist, but they never expire.
  • Trial — generous at first, but the free period ends (e.g. 12 months) and billing starts unless you act.
  • Card trap — demands a credit card for a "free" tier. Crossing a limit can bill you automatically.
  • 💳 card badge — the free tier is genuine but a card is required just to sign up (verification).
