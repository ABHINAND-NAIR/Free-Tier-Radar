Questions people actually ask

How do you verify a verdict?

By reading the provider's own pricing page and terms — not blog posts, not affiliate roundups. Anything we can't confirm doesn't get a verdict. Each entry shows the exact date it was last checked.

Do you take sponsorships or affiliate links?

No. The moment this page gets paid to rank a service, it stops being a radar and becomes an ad. Every link here points to the provider's official pricing so you can check us.

A service changed its terms — what do I do?

Tell us with the report link in the footer and it gets re-checked and re-stamped the same day. Wrong verdicts are the worst thing this page can publish, so they get fixed first.

Can I use these free tiers for my startup?

For experiments and prototypes — yes. For anything with real users, read the catch column first. Several free tiers (Vercel Hobby, for example) forbid commercial use outright. The radar exists so you find that out before the product does.

Why isn't there a rating score?

Because a single number hides the only thing that matters: how a free tier fails you. A trial and a card trap can both score 7/10 — but only one of them bills you in your sleep.

Who is behind this?

An independent project with no sponsors and no affiliate income — the pledge on the about page is the whole business model: we don't have one, so nothing here is for sale.
