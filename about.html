about this radar

Every "free" developer service ships with fine print. This page reads it once, so nobody gets the surprise bill.

That's the whole idea behind this radar. Its actually a verdict system: free forever, trial, or card trap, with the catch written in plain words and the date it was last checked. No rankings, because a single score hides exactly the thing you need to know: how a free tier fools you.

Verdicts come from reading the provider's own pricing pages and terms — not blog posts, not affiliate roundups. Anything that can't be confirmed from the source doesn't get a verdict.

THE PLEDGE

  • No affiliate links. Ever.
  • No sponsored placements. Ever.
  • Every verdict shows its check date.
  • Wrong verdicts get fixed the same day they're reported.
  • If a service pays us, we stop publishing. Full stop.

What's next

The radar grows in one direction only: more services, more categories, same honesty. An AI layer is planned — paste any pricing page URL and get the traps flagged automatically. It ships when it's verified, not before.
